## Day notes

**Day 1 — Toronto → Medina (205 km, +343 m).** Dawn start. Waterfront Trail west, around the
west end of Lake Ontario through Hamilton & St. Catharines, **cross the border at Niagara**
(passport; cross early to beat traffic), then onto the Erie Canal towpath east to Medina.
Big opener — ~11 h moving. Food idea: No Bull Burgers, Burlington.

**Day 2 — Medina → Syracuse (218 km).** Flat Erie Canal / Empire State Trail east through
Brockport, Rochester, Palmyra, Lyons. Best Western is on the **west side (by the
Fairgrounds)** — you reach it before fighting into downtown.

**Day 3 — REST (~15 km transfer).** Easy spin across town from the Fairgrounds to the
Holiday Inn Express in **East Syracuse/DeWitt** to pre-position on the east edge. Otherwise
chill — eat, sleep, check the bike.

**Day 4 — DeWitt → Albany (241 km, +241 m).** The flat epic: Mohawk Valley towpath through
Rome, Utica, Little Falls, Canajoharie, Amsterdam, Schenectady. Pancake-flat, but it's your
longest-but-one — dawn start.

**Day 5 — Albany → Brooklyn (263 km, +1,148 m). 🏁** The big one and the only hilly day —
Hudson River Greenway south (Hudson, Poughkeepsie, Beacon, Peekskill) into NYC, then over to
Brooklyn. Sunrise-to-sunset effort.
**Bail plan:** the **Metro-North Hudson Line** shadows the whole day (Poughkeepsie, Beacon,
Peekskill, Croton-Harmon → Grand Central). Bikes allowed **off-peak with a bike permit** —
roll to any station and train into the city if the legs quit.
